## Educational scope

The first 42 lessons alone were a useful gentle foundation, with most later postures still preparations. The extension adds h43 low lunge, h44 Warrior I, h45 Warrior II, h46 wall/floor downward-dog choice, h47 Sphinx, h48 knee plank, h49 staged adapted Sun A, h50 supported hinge balance, h51 coordinated locust, h52 bent-knee reverse table, h53 standing extension, h54 leg-elevation knowledge and preparation, h55–56 optional-form knowledge, h57 standing integration, h58 flow/strength blocks, h59 independent planning and h60 self-review.

This now provides selected beginner-to-intermediate practice content, not an objectively verified intermediate outcome. Supports, shorter routes, observation and omitted poses must remain honestly recorded. Reading all 60 lessons cannot establish competence in omitted balance, strength or floor work. There is no broad claim to cover all yoga traditions or qualify instructors.

## Health and media acceptance

Prone abdominal contact, prolonged supine positioning, weight-bearing, kneeling pressure and fall exposure are specifically scoped. A chair, wall or shorter variation never certifies suitability during pregnancy, injury or chronic illness. Knowledge lessons are not hidden compulsory physical tests.

Media briefs now require entries, load-bearing contacts and exits; h49 labels each adapted station and disallows invented jumps/Chaturanga. h55/h56 knowledge diagrams need instructor review before use. AI anatomy and transitions remain unverified until generated and reviewed; no current asset is an instructor demonstration or clinical validation. Final route records practice, not mastery.
